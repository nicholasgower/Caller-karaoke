## 14. Future work (post-0.1.0)

§17 is the design chapter for calling and teaching. The items below are the
short index; §17 has the reasoning, priorities, and sources.

### Caller craft

- Time-aware call variants (long / medium / short) chosen to fit the beat.
- Collision-free scheduling and first-call fit inside the four-beat count-in.
- Distance, fraction, and destination correctness (`3/4`, `1 1/4`, “to face
  your partner”).
- Call-rhythm display and a “say by” marker.
- Call support and thinning (full → standard → sparse → silent; Rescue).
- Dance and phrase validation; repeat short cards inside the 64-beat loop.
- Walkthrough and teaching mode using the dance preamble and notes.
- Loop count, ending, and band cues.
- Callability check for downloaded cards.
- Tempo labelling and range polish.

### Practice and autonomous calling

- Caller recording, playback comparison, and timing score (§17 C10).
- Cover mode, spot-check mode, and post-run self-review.
- Beat-aware TTS and speech-rate calibration (§17 C11).
- Autonomous-caller performance mode.

### Gated on a dance model

These are roadmap items only until the model in §17 C13 is designed and
accepted:

- Progression, partner/neighbor/shadow, and “new neighbor” context.
- Positional / gender-free prompt style.
- Program and set planning.
- Who/where teaching instructions and hand-availability cues.

### Platform and content

- Additional dance sources (e.g. other callers' databases) and ABC sources.
- Optional downloadable soundfont packs with clear licensing (the bundled
  subset covers the built-in instrument choices).
- Real-time tempo changes without re-rendering.
- MIDI-out to external instruments.
- More role-term presets and per-dance overrides.
- macOS and iOS builds.
- Localisation.
